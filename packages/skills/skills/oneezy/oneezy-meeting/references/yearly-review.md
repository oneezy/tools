# Yearly review

## Gather

Honor a named year, explicit dates, or a requested rolling period. When unspecified, use the current calendar year through now and state it. Calculate boundaries in the user's timezone. A named past year covers January 1 through December 31 unless the user specifies a fiscal year. For "last twelve months," calculate the requested rolling period rather than a calendar year. Ask for unknown fiscal-year boundaries.

Read goals, significant completed outcomes, unfinished work, major obstacles, and available lessons or customer feedback. Group related activity into project or business outcomes rather than listing every task. Compare goals with results only when the goals are known. Include financial results only with actual supplied evidence, and credit work to the right people.

If no usable evidence exists, ask which projects and results to review, what the goals were, and what matters most next year. If evidence is partial, write the supported review and state the coverage limit. Do not infer a whole year's performance from a few recent records.
