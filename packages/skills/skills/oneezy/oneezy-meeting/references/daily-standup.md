# Daily standup

## Gather

Use today and yesterday in the user's timezone. Gather yesterday's completed and unfinished work, today's confirmed plan, and blockers. Compare prior notes without treating an old plan as today's commitment. If no usable update exists, ask together and wait:

1. What did you do yesterday?
2. What are you doing today?
3. Is anything stopping you?

If the update is already supplied, summarize it. Mark missing sections "Not reported" unless a short follow-up is needed to choose the next action. Include unfinished work from yesterday with its actual status. Base next steps on the stated goal, dependencies, and known deadlines.

## Output

```markdown
# 🗓️ Daily standup

[Scope] · [Meeting date]

## Yesterday

1. ✅ Done: [Completed work and its result.]
2. 🟨 In progress: [Work started but not finished.]

## Today

1. 🟨 In progress: [Work already underway.]
2. 🔲 Planned: [Confirmed planned work.]

## Blockers

1. 🟥 Blocked: [Obstacle, its effect, and what would help.]

---

## Next Steps

1. [Confirmed action or a clearly labeled suggestion.]
```
