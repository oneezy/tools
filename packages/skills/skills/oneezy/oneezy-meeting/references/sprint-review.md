# Sprint review

## Gather

Accept one, two, three, or four weeks, other requested durations, named sprints, and explicit date ranges. Accept forms such as `review 3w`. If no period is supplied, use the past seven days and state it.

Read completed tasks, merged changes, releases, the user's updates, and meaningful unfinished work in the period. Deduplicate evidence for the same result. Exclude old completions with recent edits. Confirm the readiness of finished work; distinguish a finished screen from working account access and a code change from a release. Use three to seven highlights when enough results exist, but do not pad the list.

If there are no usable records, ask what was finished, what is underway, and what is stopping progress. With partial records, draft a review and note the missing coverage. Include feedback and decisions about results when supplied. Keep discussion of work-process improvements in the retrospective.

## Output

```markdown
# 📋 Sprint review

[Scope] · [Covered period]

[One short sentence describing the main result.]

## Highlights

1. ✅ Done: [Completed result and why it matters.] [Verified source link]

## In Progress

1. 🟨 In progress: [Progress and what remains before completion.]

## Blockers

1. 🟥 Blocked: [Obstacle, its effect, and what would help.]

---

## Next Steps

1. [Confirmed next action or clearly labeled suggestion.]
```

Add Feedback before the divider when feedback or decisions about the results are supplied. If there are no confirmed completions, say so in Highlights and keep unfinished progress under In Progress.
