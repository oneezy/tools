# Quarterly review

## Gather

Honor a named calendar quarter, explicit dates, or a requested rolling period. When unspecified, use the current calendar quarter through now and state it. Calculate quarter boundaries in the user's timezone. For a full named quarter, use its exact first and last dates. Do not silently substitute a full quarter for a requested three-month period.

Read the covered goals, completed outcomes, unfinished work, significant blockers, and available business or customer feedback. Use money or business metrics only when supplied by an appropriate source or the user; never infer them from GitHub activity. Compare goals with results only when the goals are known. Keep technical delivery facts tied to what clients can use.

If no usable evidence exists, ask which projects and results to review, what goals were set, and which priorities matter next. With partial evidence, prepare a factual review and note missing coverage. Make proposed next-quarter priorities distinct from agreed commitments.

## Output

```markdown
# 📊 Quarterly review

[Scope] · [Covered quarter or dates]

[One short sentence about the main results.]

## Highlights

1. ✅ Done: [Completed outcome and its value.] [Verified source link]

## Goals and Results

1. [Known goal, observed result, and what remains, or Goals not reported.]

## In Progress

1. 🟨 In progress: [Important unfinished work and remaining steps.]

## Blockers and Risks

1. [Observed obstacle or supported risk and its effect.]

## Next Quarter Priorities

1. [Confirmed priority or clearly labeled suggestion and why it matters.]

---

## Next Steps

1. [Concrete confirmed action or clearly labeled suggestion.]
```
