# Sprint retrospective

## Gather

Review the user's lessons and relevant notes from the completed work period. Honor explicit dates or a known sprint. Otherwise use the past two weeks and state that default. Ask for personal reflections that task records cannot establish. Do not infer satisfaction, motivation, blame, or agreement from activity counts.

If no useful reflections exist, ask what worked, what was difficult, and one thing the user wants to change. If records already show concrete recurring delays, offer evidence-based suggestions while labeling them as suggestions. Choose a small number of practical improvements. Assign an owner and a check-in date only when agreed or provided. Distinguish discussion from a decision.

## Output

```markdown
# 🔄 Sprint retrospective

[Scope] · [Covered period]

## What Worked

1. [Useful practice and its observed result.]

## What Was Difficult

1. [Observed problem and its effect, without assigning unsupported blame.]

## What We Learned

1. [User-reported lesson or a clearly labeled observation.]

## Suggested Improvements

1. [A small concrete change and how to tell if it helps.]

## Decisions

1. [An agreed change, or No decisions recorded.]

---

## Next Steps

1. [Confirmed action or clearly labeled suggestion, with known owner or date only.]
```
