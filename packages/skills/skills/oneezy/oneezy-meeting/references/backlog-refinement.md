# Backlog refinement

## Gather

Read current upcoming and open tasks for the requested scope. Prefer the active backlog over an assumed date window. Identify unclear requests, duplicate tasks, missing decisions, dependencies, and what would make each task ready to start. Preserve existing IDs. Combine duplicates only in the report unless editing is authorized.

If no usable backlog or update exists, ask what upcoming tasks to review and which outcome matters most. If priority or readiness is uncertain, label recommendations as suggested and ask only questions that would change the order or work. Do not invent story points or silently mark tasks ready.

## Output

```markdown
# 🗂️ Backlog refinement

[Scope] · [Meeting date]

## Goal

[The outcome these upcoming tasks should support, or Goal not reported.]

## Upcoming Work

1. [Task link or ID, plain-language result, and known priority.]

## Needs Clarification

1. [Missing detail or decision, and why it matters.]

## Dependencies and Blockers

1. [Prerequisite or obstacle, its effect, and what would help.]

## Suggested Order

1. [Task and the concrete reason to do it at this point.]

---

## Next Steps

1. [A question to resolve or an authorized next action.]
```
