# Sprint planning

## Gather

Read the current goal, ready tasks, unfinished work, known deadlines, dependencies, and available time. Use a named sprint's dates when known. If the user does not provide a planning period, propose the next two weeks and label it a suggestion. Do not pretend that period or its workload is already agreed.

Ask for a missing goal or capacity only when it changes the selection. If no useful information exists, ask what result the user wants next and how much time is available. Distinguish a suggested plan from the user's commitment. State what finishing each chosen item means. Use estimates only when supplied or requested, and label requested estimates as estimates. Do not claim that an unestimated plan fits the user's capacity.

## Output

```markdown
# 🎯 Sprint planning

[Scope] · [Confirmed or proposed period]

## Goal

[The intended result and whether it is agreed or suggested.]

## Available Time

[Known capacity or Not reported.]

## Proposed Work

1. [Task link or ID, expected result, and why it belongs in this period.]

## What Finished Means

1. [A concrete completion condition for the selected work.]

## Dependencies and Blockers

1. [Prerequisite, risk, or missing decision that affects the plan.]

## Deferred Work

1. [Work left for later and the reason, labeled Suggested when unconfirmed.]

---

## Next Steps

1. [Confirm a decision or begin confirmed work.]
```
