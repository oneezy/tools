## Decision

Build fresh rather than fork toolsview. Its useful parts are ~150 lines (the walk, the classifier shape, the single-file packaging) and its choices (cytoscape force layout, no invocation modes, no evidence, no flows) are the opposite of the brief's rules: fixed-size nodes, mode colors, entry points pinned, sequential/parallel/loop boxes, every edge traceable. skills-viewer reuses the ideas, not the code.

## Where skills-viewer still falls short

- Only `SKILL.md` bodies are scanned. Mentions inside sibling files (`setup-matt-pocock-skills/issue-tracker-github.md` says "Used by `/wayfinder`") are not edges yet; a `--files` toggle shows the files but does not read them.
- Layer 2 (LLM classification of flow relationships) is not built; flows come from ordered lists and the words "in parallel", "until", "loop", "one at a time" near a mention.
- Heuristics were tuned on this one fixture. The second fixture (pstack, Claude port) has not been run.
- Mermaid's dagre layout still produces long edges in the whole-library view; fit-to-window makes it readable, focus view is where the detail is.
